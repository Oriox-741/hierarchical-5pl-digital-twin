# Mathematical Contracts

This project uses operations-research formulations as executable constraints, benchmark definitions, and reward terms.

## LRP-MPPD-2E

The two-echelon multi-product location-routing problem with pickup and delivery defines:

- candidate hubs and operational nodes;
- primary and secondary vehicle sets;
- customer visit constraints;
- vehicle capacity constraints;
- time-window constraints;
- flow-conservation constraints;
- node activation, traversal, vehicle, and storage costs.

## MEIO

Multi-echelon inventory optimization defines:

- on-hand, reserved, in-transit, backlog, and safety stock states;
- demand mean and variance;
- lead-time mean and variance;
- service-level and bullwhip-risk indicators.

## Safety Potential

Safety Potential is a proactive risk signal. It increases before a hard failure such as stockout, collision, capacity breach, route overload, or severe service-level degradation.
