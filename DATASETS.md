# Datasets

Raw datasets are intentionally excluded from this sanitized repository.

## Public Proxy Sources Used in Evidence

- LaDe public logistics delivery data.
- NYC TLC HVFHV trip records.
- Olist Brazilian e-commerce public dataset.
- Amazon Last Mile challenge materials for route-side schema/proxy analysis where permitted by access constraints.

## What These Sources Support

They support descriptive proxy checks, route/logistics tendency inspection, replay-shape analysis, and benchmark maturity discussion.

## What They Do Not Prove

They do not validate private company operations, carrier economics, inventory economics, real dispatch failure causes, causal treatment effects, or live deployment performance. They also do not provide complete propensities/rewards/full trajectories for causal OPE.

## Raw Data Policy

No raw public or private dataset body is included. Users who want to reproduce public-data analyses must obtain datasets from their official sources and follow each source's license/terms.
